Yakovlev Design Bureau Fighters 雅科夫列夫设计局战斗机 Истребители ОКБ Яковлева

Fighter aircraft developed by the Yakovlev Design Bureau during the Second World War, including the major Yak fighter families that formed the backbone of Soviet fighter aviation.
