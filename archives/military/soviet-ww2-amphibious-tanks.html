Amphibious Tank Series 两栖坦克系列 Серия плавающих танков

Soviet amphibious light tanks developed during the interwar period. These vehicles were primarily designed for reconnaissance, communication and support missions, forming the foundation of Soviet light amphibious tank development before the Second World War.
