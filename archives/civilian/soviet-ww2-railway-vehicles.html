Soviet WWII Railway Vehicles 苏联二战铁路车辆 Советский железнодорожный подвижной состав периода Второй мировой войны

Railway vehicles used in the Soviet Union before and during the Second World War, including locomotives, passenger and freight cars, metro vehicles, multiple units and narrow-gauge railway vehicles.
