Taxi Vehicles 出租车 Такси

Taxi vehicles operated in the Soviet Union before and during the Second World War, mainly based on civilian passenger cars adapted for urban and regional taxi services.

第二次世界大战前及战争期间苏联使用的出租车辆，主要由民用轿车改装，用于城市及区域出租运输服务。
